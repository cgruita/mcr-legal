Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Missed Call Receptionist, operated by Cristian Gruita (sole proprietor), collects, uses and protects information.

What we collect

When a caller reaches the receptionist line, we collect the information the caller gives during the call, such as their name, callback number, the reason for the call and how urgent it is. We keep a transcript and a short summary of the call.

How we use it

We use this information only to notify the business owner about the call, so the owner can follow up. Messages go to the business owner's phone or email.

SMS and opt-in data

SMS opt-in data (the mobile number and the consent record) is used only to send the notifications the owner agreed to receive. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service providers

We use service providers to run the service, including Twilio (telephone and messaging), Make (automation), Google (email) and ntfy (push notifications). They process information only to provide the service.

Retention

We keep call records only as long as needed to follow up with the owner. You can ask us to delete a record at any time.

Your choices

To stop receiving texts, reply STOP. For help, reply HELP. To ask about or delete your information, contact us at gnewsletterweb@gmail.com.
